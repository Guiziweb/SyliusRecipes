  Two steps remain, they cannot be automated:

  <comment>1. Let your product variants grant tokens</>

     A pack grants tokens through a field carried by your own entity, so the
     trait has to be applied in <comment>src/Entity/Product/ProductVariant.php</>:

     <comment>use Guiziweb\SyliusTokenPlugin\Model\TokenPackInterface;
     use Guiziweb\SyliusTokenPlugin\Model\TokenPackTrait;

     class ProductVariant extends BaseProductVariant implements TokenPackInterface
     {
         use TokenPackTrait;
     }</>

     Without it, creating a token pack fails with
     <comment>Apply TokenPackTrait to your ProductVariant entity.</>

  <comment>2. Run the migrations</>

     <comment>bin/console doctrine:migrations:migrate</>

     They target MySQL, MariaDB and PostgreSQL.

  A <comment>Tokens</> section then appears in the admin menu, right below Catalog:
  create a pack under <comment>Products</> and set what your actions cost under
  <comment>Pricing</>.
